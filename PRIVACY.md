# YouRec privacy policy

Effective November 12, 2026.

**Recordings and projects**

YouRec uses the camera and microphone to record when you choose to record. Videos, audio, edits, bookmarks and subtitles are stored on your device. YouRec runs its audio filters, person detection and speech recognition on your device. The developer does not receive your recordings, voice, face information, transcripts or edits through the app.

**Optional speech model download**

If you choose automatic subtitles, YouRec downloads the speech model from Hugging Face. That download connects directly to Hugging Face and its file delivery providers. They receive the connection information needed to serve the files, including your IP address and requested file names. After installation, transcription works offline. You can remove the model in Subtitles. [Hugging Face privacy policy](https://huggingface.co/privacy)

**Photos, Files and sharing**

You choose which items to import and where to send an export or project archive. Saving to Photos adds a copy to your photo library. Files providers, sharing destinations, iCloud Photos and device backups handle any copies according to your choices and their own settings.

**Tracking and analytics**

YouRec has no advertising, analytics SDKs, developer-operated upload service or account system. The developer does not use YouRec to track you across apps or websites or sell your data.

**Retention and control**

Move a project to Recently Deleted and permanently delete it there to remove the project and its media from YouRec. You can revoke camera, microphone and Photos permissions in iOS Settings. Manage copies you exported, shared or backed up in their respective destinations.
